## About Me

I am a Junior Data Analyst focused on turning raw data into clear, actionable business insight. This portfolio brings together six skill areas that map directly to what employers look for in an entry-level analyst: querying and managing data with SQL, building reports in Excel, analyzing data with Python, applying statistical reasoning, creating visualizations and dashboards, and solving realistic business case studies end to end.
